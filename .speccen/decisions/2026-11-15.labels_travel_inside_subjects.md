# Labels stay and travel inside a `[number | text]` subject — never a bare label

[Index](../index.md)

---

|     | Info |
| --- | --- |
| **What** | Anything put to the Architect gives each question, decision, row or item a subject `[INTERNAL-NUMBER \| HUMAN UNDERSTANDABLE TEXT]`; the rule is stated once in the governing skill (Structured questions) and pointed to from each place; records (gate-check, delegated-decision and interview tables, Retrospective findings, the fix-attempt log line) keep their form. Delegate, Phase 03 (03/2, 03/3; DD-10, DD-11); adopted by the Architect: "yes" (review R/1, R/2, R/29, 2026-11-15). |
| **Why** | The labels make every decision traceable across the log, the delegated decisions and the Retrospective; the Architect could not answer a stop round written in bare labels ("do you really think a human knows what DD-*, P*, etc ... is?", issue #26). |
| **Alternatives** | Drop the labels — loses traceability. A glossary of labels — still sends the Architect to another place. Plain words with the number as a trailing pointer — the Architect answers by number, so the number must lead. Fix it in chat only — the stop record in `summary.md` must read on its own too (issue #26, §4). |
