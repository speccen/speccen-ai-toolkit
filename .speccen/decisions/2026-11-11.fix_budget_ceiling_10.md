# The fix budget is at most 10 fix attempts; an invalid value counts as 1

[Index](../index.md)

---

|     | Info |
| --- | --- |
| **What** | `fixes` takes `1` – `10`, default `1`. The delegate proposed a ceiling of 3 (DD-12); the Architect overrode it: "1 max loop 10" (review R/1, 2026-11-11). |
| **Why** | The Architect wants the delegate to loop until the issues are fixed ("I don't know how to express in simple English that have to loop until issues are fixed", 2026-11-11) and set the ceiling at 10. The progress guard and the conclusions guard stop a loop that does not converge. |
| **Alternatives** | A ceiling of 3 (the issue #25 brief, §2 guard 3) — overridden; no ceiling — not chosen. |
