# A fix that only repairs how a decision is recorded is not a stop

[Index](../index.md)

---

|     | Info |
| --- | --- |
| **What** | Stop condition 5 stops on a fix that changes a phase conclusion, an ADR, or what a delegated decision decides. Splitting a question into one per decision with the same answer each, moving a question to the round its dependencies allow, correcting a citation, or raising a decision's materiality to `MATERIAL — unconfirmed` is not such a change; each repaired entry keeps its answer, states the repair, and still goes to the review round. The Architect: "include and apply this immediately in this refactoring" (03/16, 2026-11-13); promoted in review R/30 (2026-11-15). |
| **Why** | Under `autonomous` + `stops: proceed` the run stopped on questions whose proposed answer was a plain "yes" — record repairs with nothing for the Architect to decide. |
| **Alternatives** | Keep any change to a delegated-decision record a stop — not chosen: it stops runs for bookkeeping. Widen it to fixes that change an answer — rejected: an answer is the Architect's or the delegate's decision, not a record. |
