# Questions to the Architect: [number | text] subjects, never a bare label — Business Outcome Brief (intent, seed)

## Capability To Be Delivered
- Each question, decision or item that speccen puts to the Architect has a number and a plain text, for example `[DD-4 | Does the fix budget also cover Phase 05's own fix rule?]`.
- The Architect answers a stop or a review from the message. The Architect does not open other files first.
- A correction that only changes how a decision is recorded does not stop an autonomous run.

## Risk Delta (expected)
- Risk this closes: the Architect answers a question without knowing its subject, or loses time to find it.
- Risk this closes: an autonomous run stops for a record correction that has a clear answer.
- Risk accepted: messages to the Architect become longer, because each label has its text.
- Risk accepted: the delegate makes record corrections without the Architect. The review round still shows each one.

## What It Will Unblock
- Faster answers at stops and in the review round.
- Fewer stops in autonomous runs.

- TCO / cost / budget: out of scope for this feature-level brief.
