# Questions to the Architect: [number | text] subjects, never a bare label — Business Outcome Brief (seed)

Intent: [Phase 04 executive seed](../phase-04-specification/doc-executive.md). Intended → delivered: the same.

## Capability Delivered
- Each question, decision or item that speccen puts to the Architect has a number and a plain text. The Architect answers from the message.
- A correction that only changes how a decision is recorded does not stop an autonomous run.

## Risk Delta
- Risk closed: the Architect answers a question without knowing its subject.
- Risk closed: an autonomous run stops for a record correction with a clear answer.
- Risk accepted: messages to the Architect are longer.
- Risk accepted: the delegate makes record corrections without the Architect. The review round shows each one.

## What It Unblocks
- Faster answers at stops and in the review round.
- Fewer stops in autonomous runs.

## Scope Note
- TCO / cost / budget: out of scope for this feature-level brief.
